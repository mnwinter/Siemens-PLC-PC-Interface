## Stop and safety boundary

The composed pusher uses its configured 1.38 m centre height, grounded mounts,
outboard guided shafts and moving plate fasteners. A rigid two-arm yoke extends
the plate 879.73 mm from the carriage to contact the carton. The frame is set
back 250 mm to clear conveyor feet; the 1.35 m stroke is unchanged. Front bolts and lettering are recessed to
avoid penetrating the load. The pusher is offset along the conveyor to clear
the inclined photoeye path. Its configured optical heights are 0.847 m and 2.405 m
heights; the optional visual carton-centre datum positions the body across the
beam at first detection without changing the symbolic plant model.

The flat receiving table meets the belt edge at the same 900 mm top height.
Its supports clear the conveyor bracing, and the receiver-side photoeye stand
is beyond the table. The canonical plant releases the infeed carton at 80%
stroke. The renderer keeps that carton visible, continues it to the table at
full stroke, and holds it during retraction. The same single rendered carton
returns to the infeed when the plant admits its next load; this does not model
a queue of received cartons. Reset restores the staged carton.

Plate contact, connected yoke travel, support and solid clearance have sampled
geometry checks and actual-ladder contact checks. Native Windows inspection
covers intermediate extension, actual transfer, full extension and intermediate
retraction in FR/FL/RL/RR/Top held views, plus retention, Stop/Reset and resumed
real-time playback. Rear views partly hide the low sensor; close views crop
peripheral equipment ends and feet, so these views accept the central transfer
geometry only.
The inclined beam clears above the received carton. Continuous lens-to-lens
geometry agrees with feedback through reference ladder cycles; fine stroke
sampling allows a 2 mm grazing boundary at the canonical 80% release. Head
aim, grounded supports and cable connections also pass geometric checks.
The low stand is outside the rail and cable routes. Nine static enclosing-box
candidates are excluded by cable triangle bounds; complete physical transfer
remains unproven.
These checks do not establish a rated mechanism or safety function.

A normal Stop removes PLC-owned commands according to the scene runtime. This document does not prove a safety function, a real E-stop circuit, a PLC watchdog, or live-machine commissioning.

## Offline inspection controls

Launch with `-- --app-shell --visual-scene-review --shell-scene=scene-2-conveyor-pusher
--shell-view=operator`. Hold offline plant clock freezes the local controller
and equipment. Step 0.1 s advances five existing 20 ms scans; Step 20 ms
advances one. Neither step advances a stopped controller. The QA label reports
time, actual stroke and completed count. Releasing Hold restores real-time
playback; editor, scene and execution-source changes release it.

The inspected three-rung QA reference was generated by
`-- --verify-plant-motion --visual-scene-review` into ignored
`.tools/plant-review-scene2.rpproj.json` and loaded through normal File -> Open
Ladder Agent Project. It is separate from the default empty training exercise.
Native checkpoints: scan 58 / 53.3% extending; 63 / 86.7% / count 1 at transfer;
65 / 100% extended; 72 / 53.3% retracting; 80 / 0% retained on the table.
The rounded 80.0% display at scan 62 still had count 0: the canonical strict
floating-point threshold was crossed at the next scan. This inspector leaves
the existing threshold and model semantics unchanged. The single carton
remained on the table through scan 180 and recycled to the infeed at scan 188
(3.76 s), the canonical next-load boundary. This is a prescribed symbolic
sequence, not collision, pressure or gravity simulation.
